# Offline verb catalogue — modified dictionary data

English: 7,100 distinct verbs and verb expressions. Portuguese: 2,415 distinct
infinitives and verb expressions. Counts are lemmas, not inflected forms.

Princeton WordNet 3.0 © 2006 Princeton University (WORDNET-LICENSE).
Spanish equivalents: Multilingual Central Repository 3.0, via Open Multilingual
WordNet 1.4 (CC BY 3.0, SPANISH-LICENSE).
Portuguese lemmas: OpenWordnet-PT, Alexandre Rademaker and contributors,
https://github.com/arademaker/openWordnet-PT (CC BY-SA 3.0,
PORTUGUESE-LICENSE). English and Spanish equivalents are joined by WordNet
synset. OMW is curated by Francis Bond and contributors: https://omwn.org/.

These JSON adaptations are licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0, with the above source
notices retained. Changes: verb-only extraction, Portuguese infinitive filtering,
Spanish sense alignment, JSON conversion, alphabetical ordering. Full preserved
inputs, checksums and regeneration script are in external-data/wordnet/ and
scripts/build-verb-catalog.py in this public repository.

Dictionaries may contain rare, regional or dated senses. Spanish equivalents
are sense-specific aids, not interchangeable translations in every sentence.
No conjugations or invented usage examples are generated from these lemmas.
